Conflicting Content-Length and Transfer-Encoding no longer permit persistent reuse or protocol switches, and received requests and responses strip the overridden Content-Length before exposure to forwarding callers. Sending strips forbidden framing headers from informational, 204, and successful CONNECT responses.
Chunked writers now reject forbidden framing, routing, and hop-by-hop trailer fields instead of serializing dangerous trailer metadata.
Received informational and 204 responses now hide forbidden framing headers from forwarding callers, including when only one of those headers was present.
Outgoing chunked trailers also reject authentication, content-format, conditional, and response-control fields that must be available in the header section.
